Customer reference request checklist

Use this public-safe checklist before sales, customer marketing, advocacy, CS, or founder-led teams ask a customer to serve as a reference. Do not paste CRM exports, customer lists, private contacts, personal emails, contract details, credentials, or private account data into public tools.

Request Context

  • Deal context, stage, or reference purpose.
  • Buyer persona, industry, use case, or match rationale.
  • Customer advocate fit and relationship health.
  • Consent, permission, approval status, or approval path.

Advocate Guardrails

  • Recent asks, reference fatigue, rotation, or cool-down period.
  • Time-boxed ask and expected commitment.
  • Confidentiality boundary, logo/name/quote permission, and disclosure notes.
  • Owner, reviewer, next step, and follow-up path.

Safety

  • Use respectful optional wording; make it easy to decline.
  • Avoid pressure, entitlement, fake proof, paid-review ambiguity, and unsupported sales claims.
  • Keep CRM exports, customer lists, private contacts, contract details, credentials, and customer identifiers out of public handoffs.
